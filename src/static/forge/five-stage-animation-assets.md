# Five-stage cartoon animations

Stage 1 and 2 use the existing cartoon furnace and hammer imagegen sprite sheets. The prompt set is recorded in cartoon-animation-prompts.md.

Stage 3: `src/static/forge/quench-cartoon-v1.webp`

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool:

Use case: stylized-concept. Production animation sprite sheet for a cozy Chinese educational blacksmith app. Generate a SQUARE image containing an EXACT 2 by 2 grid of FOUR animation frames in equal square cells. True transparent alpha background across all four cells. Identical orthographic camera, object scale, baseline and anchor across ALL four frames, every object stays INSIDE its cell with 12 percent safe padding. No grid, borders, frame labels, numbers, lettering, watermark, text or UI. Clear hand-drawn 2D CARTOON ANIMATION art, clean thick dark cocoa outlines, simple rounded silhouettes, flat cel shading, muted terracotta and caramel wood, pale butter highlights. Cozy storybook game art; NOT photorealistic, NOT metallic realistic rendering, NOT detailed 3D, NO elaborate textures. Readable at 180 pixels. No characters, human hands or human body parts. A SHORT simple sword with warm caramel handle, modest golden crossguard and broad blade, same design across frames. Scene: cooling a hot sword in a compact round wooden water barrel. Barrel fixed in lower half, warm copper hoops, small muted slate blue water surface visible. Four frames in reading order top-left/top-right/bottom-left/bottom-right: 1 hot orange sword held point down above water, angled slightly right, handle above barrel; 2 blade partly dips into water with a few pale cream steam puffs; 3 blade immersed deeper with a small splash, circular water ripples and round gentle steam puffs; 4 blade lifted back above barrel with COOL pale-silver blade, two water droplets, steam settling. Keep barrel size, position and appearance identical. Sword moves vertically along SAME line. Steam remains inside cell. No dramatic explosion. Square 1024 by1024.

Stage 4: `src/static/forge/grind-cartoon-v1.webp`

Authored as an editable native SVG sprite sheet, `src/static/forge/grind-cartoon-v1.svg`, after the image generation service rejected the grinding-sheet request. Rasterized for the same four-frame player. This animation uses a rotating grindstone, moving blade and contact sparks.
