# Cartoon forge animation assets

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Sprite sheets contain four frames in a 2 × 2 grid, used by AnimatedForgeScene.vue. WebP files preserve the generated transparency; PNG originals remain in the generated image archive.

No people, tools, floating material or trails outside the furnace. Animation frame order reading top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right: 1 quiet orange flame, 2 slightly taller dancing flame heating the sword, 3 brief buttery yellow flare at furnace opening with 3 tiny sparks INSIDE the cell, 4 warm settled orange flame and glowing sword. Structure stays exactly anchored and identical, only flame shape and glow change. Tight compact full furnace silhouette in every cell. 1024 by 1024 square sprite sheet.
